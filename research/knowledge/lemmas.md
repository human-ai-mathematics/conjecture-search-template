# Reusable lemmas

Compact facts used across more than one node, each with the guardrail that limits it. A lemma
without its hypotheses is a trap: state what it needs, and state where it stops being true.

A fact belongs here once it has been used twice. Until then it lives in the exploration that
produced it.

## Template

### `<short name>`

**Statement.** The fact, precisely, in LaTeX `$...$`.

**Guardrail.** The hypotheses it needs and the regime where it fails. Name the first thing that
breaks.

**Used by.** Ledger nodes or dossiers that rely on it.

**Source.** The dossier, exploration, or citation it comes from. A lemma promoted from an
exploration is not thereby certified: if a proof depends on it, it needs a node and a dossier.
